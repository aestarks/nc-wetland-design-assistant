    Equations, Design Rules & Credible Sources

    1. Runoff Volume (Simple Method — Schueler / NC DEQ Stormwater Design Manual)

    Rv = 0.05 + 0.009 × I (where I is percent imperviousness, 0–100)

    V_runoff (ft³) = (P / 12) × Rv × A_drainage (ft²)

    2. Wetland Surface Area & Storage Sizing

    Maximum temporary ponding depth above permanent pool: d_temp ≤ 15 in (1.25 ft).

    Required wetland surface area at maximum ponding depth: A_req (ft²) = V_runoff / 1.25 ft.

    Actual temporary ponding depth on proposed site: d_actual (in) = (V_runoff / A_available) × 12.

    3. Conceptual Geometry (3:1 Length-to-Width Ratio)

    To prevent hydraulic short-circuiting: W = √(A_design / 3), L = 3 × W.

    4. Zone Allocation & Depth Standards

    • Forebay: 12.5% of surface area (target 10–15%), 24–40 in below permanent pool (design: 36 in).
    • Non-Forebay Deep Pools: 10% of surface area (target 5–15%), ≥18 in below permanent pool (design: 24 in).
    • Shallow-Water Zone: 40% of surface area (target 35–45%), 0–9 in below permanent pool (design: 6 in).
    • Temporary-Inundation Zone: 37.5% of surface area (target 30–45%), 0–15 in above permanent pool.

    5. Drawdown & Planting Density

    • Drawdown Time: Target 2–5 days (48–120 hours) via orifice/weir outlet control. Average outflow rate Q_avg = V_runoff / (T_drawdown × 86,400).
    • Shallow-Water Planting Density: Minimum 50 herbaceous plugs per 200 ft² (0.25 plants/ft²).
    • Embankment / Fill Slopes: Non-clumping turf grass only (no trees or woody shrubs).

    6. Data & Regulatory Sources

    • Rainfall Data: NOAA Atlas 14 Volume 2 (Precipitation-Frequency Atlas of the United States, North Carolina).
    • Design Standards: NC Department of Environmental Quality (NC DEQ) Stormwater Design Manual — Chapter C-4 (Constructed Stormwater Wetlands).
    • Plant & Invasive Screening: NC State Extension Gardener Plant Toolbox & NC Invasive Plant Council (NC-IPC).
